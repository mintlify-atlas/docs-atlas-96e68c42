## Overview

The References section is the final data section of your evaluation. You'll need to provide both **professional references** (work-related) and **personal references** (people who know you personally but are not family).

<Info>
This is section 6 of 6. Completing this section means all your data capture is finished!
</Info>

## Accessing References Form

From your evaluation dashboard, click on **References** (Referencias). You'll be directed to:

```
/captureData/referenciasCapture/[encrypted_evaluation_id]
```

## Before You Begin

Gather contact information for:

### Professional References (3 required)
- Full names
- Job titles
- Company names
- Phone numbers (work phones preferred)

### Personal References (3 required)
- Full names
- Home or mobile phone numbers (both if available)

<Note>
**Important reference guidelines:**
- References should NOT be family members
- References should be able to vouch for your character
- Make sure to ask permission before listing someone
- Provide accurate, current contact information
- References may be contacted during background investigation
</Note>

## Part 1: Professional References (Laborales)

You need three work-related references.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Your Professional References">
    Good choices include:
    - Current or former supervisors
    - Colleagues or coworkers
    - HR personnel who know you
    - Clients or business contacts
    - Mentors in your field

    <Warning>
    Do NOT use:
    - Family members
    - People you've never actually worked with
    - Friends pretending to be coworkers
    - Anyone who would give you a poor reference
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reference #1 Information">
    **Nombre** (`referenciasNombre1`)
    - Full name of the reference
    - Include professional title if customary (Ing., Lic., etc.)

    **Puesto** (`referenciasPuesto1`)
    - Their job title or position
    - Example: "Supervisor de Área", "Director de Recursos Humanos"

    **Empresa** (`referenciasEmpresa1`)
    - Name of the company or organization
    - Where this person works (or worked with you)

    **Teléfono** (`referenciasTelefono1`)
    - Phone number where they can be reached
    - Work number preferred
    - Include extension if applicable
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reference #2 Information">
    Same fields for second professional reference:
    - Nombre (`referenciasNombre2`)
    - Puesto (`referenciasPuesto2`)
    - Empresa (`referenciasEmpresa2`)
    - Teléfono (`referenciasTelefono2`)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reference #3 Information">
    Same fields for third professional reference:
    - Nombre (`referenciasNombre3`)
    - Puesto (`referenciasPuesto3`)
    - Empresa (`referenciasEmpresa3`)
    - Teléfono (`referenciasTelefono3`)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Part 2: Personal References (Personales)

You need three personal references.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Your Personal References">
    Good choices include:
    - Long-time friends
    - Neighbors who know you well
    - Community leaders (priests, coaches, etc.)
    - Teachers or mentors
    - Landlords (if you rent)

    <Warning>
    Do NOT use:
    - Family members (parents, siblings, spouse, in-laws, cousins)
    - People you barely know
    - Anyone under 18 years old
    - People with serious criminal records
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Personal Reference #1">
    **Nombre** (`referenciasNombreP1`)
    - Full name of the reference

    **Teléfono Fijo** (`referenciasFijoP1`)
    - Home landline phone
    - Can leave blank if they don't have one

    **Teléfono Celular** (`referenciasCelularP1`)
    - Mobile phone number
    - Preferred for contact

    <Info>
    Provide at least one phone number (landline or mobile) for each reference. Having both is better.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Personal Reference #2">
    Same fields for second personal reference:
    - Nombre (`referenciasNombreP2`)
    - Teléfono Fijo (`referenciasFijoP2`)
    - Teléfono Celular (`referenciasCelularP2`)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Personal Reference #3">
    Same fields for third personal reference:
    - Nombre (`referenciasNombreP3`)
    - Teléfono Fijo (`referenciasFijoP3`)
    - Teléfono Celular (`referenciasCelularP3`)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Saving Your References

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review All Information">
    Before saving, double-check:
    - All names are spelled correctly
    - Phone numbers are accurate and complete
    - You have 3 professional AND 3 personal references
    - No family members are listed
    - All fields are filled out (except optional landlines)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save References">
    Click the save button.

    Success message: **"Referencias guardadas con exito!"**

    You'll be redirected back to your evaluation dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify Completion">
    On your dashboard, you should now see:
    - References section marked as complete ✓
    - Progress at 100% (6 of 6 sections complete)
    - All six data sections showing green checkmarks
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing References

### Updating References

To change reference information:

1. Return to the References section from your dashboard
2. The form will be pre-populated with your existing references
3. Make any necessary changes
4. Click update/save
5. Success message: **"Referencias guardadas con exito!"**

<Note>
You can update references at any time before finalizing your evaluation. After finalization, changes may not be possible.
</Note>

### Deleting All References

From the evaluation dashboard:

1. Delete the entire References section
2. Confirm deletion
3. Success message: **"Datos referencias eliminados"**
4. You'll need to re-enter all 6 references from scratch

<Warning>
Deleting references removes all 6 at once. There's no way to delete individual references - you must delete all and re-enter.
</Warning>

## Best Practices

### Choosing References

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Professional References - Best Choices">
    **Priority Order:**
    1. **Direct supervisors** - Best choice if you have a good relationship
    2. **HR professionals** - Can verify employment and character
    3. **Senior colleagues** - Who have worked closely with you
    4. **Department heads** - Who know your work
    5. **Former bosses** - If current supervisor isn't available

    **For Permanencia (existing employees):**
    - Your current immediate supervisor (strongly recommended)
    - Previous supervisors within the agency
    - Colleagues in positions of authority

    **For Nuevo Ingreso (new hires):**
    - Recent supervisors from previous jobs
    - Employers from last 2-3 jobs
    - Colleagues who can vouch for your work
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Personal References - Best Choices">
    **Priority Order:**
    1. **Long-term friends** (5+ years) who know you well
    2. **Neighbors** you interact with regularly
    3. **Community leaders** (religious, civic, sports)
    4. **Mentors** from outside work
    5. **Former teachers** (if recent graduate)

    **Characteristics of good personal references:**
    - Know you for at least 2-3 years
    - Can speak to your character and behavior
    - Are responsible, reliable people themselves
    - Will answer calls from investigators
    - Can provide specific examples about you
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="References to Avoid">
    **Never use:**
    - Family members (automatic rejection)
    - People you've had conflicts with
    - Anyone who might give negative feedback
    - People who barely know you
    - References you haven't asked first
    - Anyone who is difficult to reach
    - People with poor reputations
    - Minors (under 18)

    **Questionable choices:**
    - Friends who are also applying (not ideal)
    - References used by many other applicants
    - People who have moved far away
    - Anyone with language barriers
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Contact Information Best Practices

1. **Verify phone numbers**: Call them yourself before submitting
2. **Get permission**: Always ask before listing someone
3. **Provide multiple numbers**: Work and mobile if possible
4. **Update if changed**: Check contact info is still current
5. **Time zones**: Note if reference is in different time zone
6. **Best times to call**: Inform investigators if there are ideal calling times

### What to Tell Your References

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Notify Your References">
    **Tell them:**
    - You've listed them as a reference
    - What organization is conducting the evaluation (CEACC Durango)
    - They may receive a call for a background check
    - The call may come from an unknown number
    - When they should expect contact (after you finalize)

    **Ask them:**
    - If they're comfortable being a reference
    - To answer honestly about their experience with you
    - To have your contact information handy
    - To return calls if they miss the investigator
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What References Will Be Asked">
    Professional references may be asked:
    - How long they've known you
    - Your job duties and performance
    - Your attendance and punctuality
    - Your relationships with coworkers
    - Strengths and weaknesses
    - Whether they'd rehire or recommend you
    - Any disciplinary issues

    Personal references may be asked:
    - How long they've known you
    - The nature of your relationship
    - Your character and behavior
    - Your reliability and trustworthiness
    - Any concerns about you in a law enforcement role
    - Your reputation in the community
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Special Scenarios

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Nuevo Ingreso - No Work History">
    **Situation:** You've never worked before.

    **Solution for Professional References:**
    - Use professors or teachers
    - School counselors or advisors
    - Internship supervisors
    - Volunteer coordinators
    - Community program leaders
    - Any professional who knows your work ethic

    <Note>
    The key is people who can speak to how you perform in structured environments, even if not traditional employment.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Self-Employed or Business Owner">
    **Situation:** You work for yourself.

    **Solution for Professional References:**
    - Major clients or customers
    - Business partners or associates
    - Suppliers or vendors you work with
    - Professional organization contacts
    - Accountant or business attorney
    - Previous employer before self-employment
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Recent Arrival to Area">
    **Situation:** You just moved to Durango.

    **Solution for Personal References:**
    - Landlord or property manager
    - New coworkers (if you've started working)
    - People from organizations you've joined
    - Neighbors you've met
    - Friends/references from previous location are acceptable

    <Info>
    References don't have to be local, but they should be reachable by phone.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Bad Work Experience">
    **Situation:** You had problems with a previous employer.

    **Solution:**
    - Don't use that employer as a reference
    - Use other supervisors or coworkers from that job who will speak positively
    - Use references from other jobs
    - If your most recent job, be prepared to explain the gap or issue honestly
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Large Time Gap in Employment">
    **Situation:** Haven't worked in years.

    **Solution for Professional References:**
    - Use former employers (even if several years ago)
    - If homemaker: use volunteer work supervisors
    - If student: use professors
    - If caregiver: use healthcare coordinators or family references are acceptable in this specific case
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Validation and Requirements

### Required Information

**For Each Professional Reference:**
- Name (required)
- Position (required)
- Company (required)
- Phone number (required)

**For Each Personal Reference:**
- Name (required)
- At least one phone number (landline OR mobile, both preferred)

### Common Validation Errors

- **Duplicate references**: Can't use the same person twice
- **Family members**: System may flag family surnames
- **Missing phone numbers**: At least one number required per reference
- **Incomplete forms**: All 6 references must be filled

## Troubleshooting

### Can't save references

**Problem:** Save button doesn't work.

**Solution:**
- Ensure all 6 references are filled out
- Check that all professional references have all 4 fields
- Check that all personal references have at least one phone number
- Refresh page and try again

### Reference information wrong

**Problem:** Made a mistake in a reference's info.

**Solution:**
- Return to References section
- Update the incorrect information
- Save again - this updates the existing data

### Reference won't answer calls

**Problem:** Investigator can't reach a reference.

**Solution:**
- Update the contact information if you have better numbers
- Contact your reference and ask them to watch for calls
- If the reference is unavailable, you may need to update with a different reference

### Used a family member by mistake

**Problem:** Listed a family member as personal reference.

**Solution:**
- Update immediately before finalization
- Replace with a non-family member
- If discovered during investigation, it may delay or negatively impact your evaluation
