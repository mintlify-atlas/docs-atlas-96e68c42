## Overview

After completing all six data sections, you must upload supporting documents. The specific documents required depend on your evaluation type: **Permanencia** or **Nuevo Ingreso**.

<Warning>
Your evaluation CANNOT be finalized until all required documents are uploaded. Start gathering these documents early in the process.
</Warning>

## General File Requirements

Before uploading any document:

### File Format
- **Accepted formats**: PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG
- **Recommended**: PDF for multi-page documents
- **Not accepted**: Word documents, Excel files, text files

### File Size
- **Maximum size**: Check the system (typically 5-10 MB per file)
- **Recommended**: Keep files under 5 MB for faster upload
- **Tip**: Compress large PDFs or images if needed

### Quality Requirements
- **Readable**: All text must be legible
- **Complete**: Entire document visible, nothing cut off
- **Clear**: No blur, good lighting, proper resolution
- **Correct orientation**: Right-side up, not sideways

<Warning>
Poor quality uploads may be rejected, requiring you to re-upload. Take time to ensure documents are clear and complete.
</Warning>

### Scanning Tips

1. **Use a scanner**: Better quality than phone photos when possible
2. **Phone camera alternative**: If no scanner, use a scanning app
3. **Lighting**: Use natural light or bright indoor lighting
4. **Flat surface**: Press documents flat, remove wrinkles
5. **Background**: Use a dark background for better contrast
6. **Multiple pages**: Save as single PDF when possible

## Required Documents: Permanencia

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="1. Birth Certificate (Acta de Nacimiento)">
    **What to upload:**
    - Official birth certificate issued by Civil Registry
    - Must be recent (within last 3-6 months for some purposes)
    - Certified copy (copia certificada)

    **Requirements:**
    - Full document, both sides if printed on both sides
    - Legible text
    - Official stamps and signatures visible
    - Shows your full legal name, date and place of birth, parents' names

    **Common issues:**
    - Expired certificate (get a new one)
    - Damaged or faded document (request replacement)
    - Name doesn't match other documents (may need correction)

    <Note>
    If your name on the birth certificate differs from your current legal name, you may need additional documentation explaining the change.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="2. Official ID (INE / Credencial de Elector)">
    **What to upload:**
    - Valid INE (Instituto Nacional Electoral) card
    - Both front AND back

    **Requirements:**
    - Current, not expired
    - Photo clearly visible
    - All text readable
    - Holographic security features visible (if scanning)
    - Both sides in same file or clearly labeled if separate

    **Common issues:**
    - Expired INE (renew immediately)
    - Address on INE doesn't match current address (explain in personal data)
    - Damaged card (request replacement)

    <Info>
    Upload front and back as separate images, or combine into one PDF with both pages.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="3. Proof of Address (Comprobante de Domicilio)">
    **What to upload:**
    Recent utility bill or official document showing your current address

    **Acceptable documents:**
    - Electricity bill (CFE)
    - Water bill
    - Natural gas bill
    - Phone bill (landline)
    - Internet/cable bill
    - Property tax statement (predial)
    - Bank statement (last month)

    **Requirements:**
    - Must be from last 3 months (some accept up to 6 months)
    - Shows your full name
    - Shows complete address matching your Personal Data section
    - Shows issue date clearly
    - Official letterhead or company logo visible

    **Common issues:**
    - Bill in someone else's name (see solutions below)
    - Too old (get recent bill)
    - Address doesn't match exactly (verify accuracy)

    <Tip>
    **If bill is in someone else's name:**
    - Include a letter from the bill holder stating you live there
    - Or use property lease agreement
    - Or use official mail addressed to you
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="4. Proof of Studies (Comprobante de Estudios)">
    **What to upload:**
    Documentation of your highest educational degree

    **Acceptable documents:**
    - Degree certificate (Certificado)
    - Diploma
    - Official transcript (Historial Académico)
    - Professional license (Cédula Profesional)
    - Title (Título)

    **Requirements:**
    - Matches the degree you listed in Academic Data section
    - Official document from the institution
    - Shows your name, degree, institution, date
    - Original or certified copy

    **Common issues:**
    - Lost certificate (contact school for duplicate)
    - Degree in process (upload most recent transcript with note)
    - Foreign degree (may need validation)

    <Note>
    The document must match what you entered in the Academic Data section. If you listed a Licenciatura, upload your Licenciatura certificate.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="5. Proof of Income (Comprobante de Ingresos)">
    **What to upload:**
    Official documentation of your current income

    **Acceptable documents:**
    - Recent pay stub (last month)
    - Official salary statement
    - Form from employer (HR department)
    - Last tax declaration
    - Bank statement showing salary deposits

    **Requirements:**
    - Shows your name
    - Shows amount of income
    - Shows employer name
    - Recent (last 1-3 months)
    - Official letterhead or stamps

    **Common issues:**
    - Self-employed (upload tax declaration or client contracts)
    - Multiple income sources (upload for primary job)
    - Informal employment (get letter from employer)

    <Info>
    For government employees, an official salary statement from your HR department is ideal.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Required Documents: Nuevo Ingreso

Nuevo Ingreso requires all the Permanencia documents PLUS additional ones:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Documents 1-4: Same as Permanencia">
    - Birth Certificate
    - Official ID (INE)
    - Proof of Address
    - Proof of Studies

    See the Permanencia section above for details on these documents.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="5. Military Service Card (Cartilla Militar)">
    **What to upload:**
    - Military service card (Cartilla del Servicio Militar Nacional)
    - Complete document

    **Requirements:**
    - Shows your photo
    - Shows full name matching other documents
    - Shows military service status (Liberado/Released)
    - All pages if multi-page
    - Legible text and stamps

    **Gender-specific:**
    - **Men**: Required (unless exempt due to age)
    - **Women**: Not required (system checks gender from registration)

    **Common issues:**
    - Never obtained (must get one if eligible age)
    - Lost (request duplicate from military)
    - In process (upload temporary document with explanation)

    <Warning>
    Men who should have military service but don't may face employment restrictions. Resolve this before applying.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="6. Proof of Income OR Motive Description">
    **Option A: Proof of Income (if employed)**
    - Same as Permanencia #5 above
    - Upload recent pay stub or salary statement

    **Option B: Motive Description (if unemployed)**
    - If you're not currently employed, you must provide a written motive
    - Explain why you're applying for this position
    - Describe your interest in law enforcement/public service
    - Explain your current situation (recent graduate, career change, etc.)

    **Motive Description Requirements:**
    - Type a clear explanation (at least a paragraph)
    - Be sincere and professional
    - Address why you want this specific role
    - Mention how you support yourself currently (family, savings, etc.)

    <Info>
    The system has a special form for entering the motive description if you're not uploading an income document.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Optional Documents

Both evaluation types have optional documents that can strengthen your application:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Photo of Evaluado (Optional but Recommended)">
    - Recent photograph of yourself
    - Professional appearance
    - Clear, good lighting
    - Passport-style photo acceptable
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Bank Account Statements (Optional)">
    - Supports your Economic Data section
    - Shows financial responsibility
    - Include for major accounts mentioned in Economic Data
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Credit Bureau Report (Optional)">
    - Recent credit report from Buró de Crédito
    - Shows credit history and score
    - Demonstrates financial transparency
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Property Documents (Optional)">
    - Deed (Escritura) for real estate you own
    - Vehicle title (Título de propiedad)
    - Supports assets listed in Economic Data
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Additional Income Documentation (Optional)">
    - Rental agreements if you have rental income
    - Business licenses if self-employed
    - Contracts showing other income sources
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Residence Documentation (Optional)">
    - Photo of home facade
    - Rental agreement or lease
    - Property deed
    - Utility setup documents
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Spouse Documentation (Optional)">
    - Marriage certificate
    - Spouse's income documentation
    - Especially important if spouse contributes to household income
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Weapon License (Optional)">
    - If you indicated you carry a weapon in Career Trajectory
    - Official weapon permit
    - Registration documents
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Legal Proceedings Documentation (Optional)">
    - If you disclosed legal issues in Career Trajectory
    - Resolution documents
    - Acquittal or dismissal notices
    - Proof of completed sanctions
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Info>
Optional documents are not required to finalize your evaluation, but they support your claims in the data sections and demonstrate transparency.
</Info>

## How to Upload Documents

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Document Upload">
    From your evaluation dashboard, click on the document you want to upload.

    You'll be directed to:
    ```
    /fileUploadView/[document_id]/[encrypted_evaluation_id]
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review Document Information">
    The page will show:
    - Document name
    - Description of what's needed
    - Whether it's required or optional
    - Any special instructions
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Your File">
    - Click the file selection button
    - Browse to your document
    - Select the file
    - Verify it's the correct document
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload">
    - Click the upload button
    - Wait for upload to complete
    - Don't close the page during upload

    Success message: **"Documento cargado con exito"**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify Upload">
    - You'll be redirected to your evaluation dashboard
    - The document should now show as uploaded ✓
    - You should be able to view or download what you uploaded
  </Step>

  <Step title="Repeat for All Required Documents">
    Continue uploading until all required documents show green checkmarks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Uploaded Documents

### Viewing Uploaded Documents

From your evaluation dashboard:
- Uploaded documents show with a checkmark ✓
- Click on a document to view details
- You can preview or download what you uploaded

### Replacing a Document

If you need to upload a different file:

1. Go to the document section
2. Delete the existing upload
3. Upload the new file
4. Success message: **"Documento cargado con exito"**

### Deleting a Document

1. Go to the document page
2. Click the delete button
3. Confirm deletion
4. Success message: **"Documento borrado con exito"**
5. The document requirement will show as incomplete again

<Warning>
Be careful when deleting. You'll need to upload a replacement to complete your evaluation.
</Warning>

## Motive Description (Nuevo Ingreso Only)

If you're applying as Nuevo Ingreso without current employment:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Motive Form">
    Instead of uploading an income document, look for the motive description option.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write Your Motive">
    **Motivo de Evaluación** (`motivoDescripcion`)
    
    Write a clear explanation including:
    - Why you want to work in law enforcement
    - Your interest in public service
    - Your relevant skills or background
    - Your current situation (student, job seeker, etc.)
    - How you're currently supporting yourself

    **Example:**
    "Deseo ingresar a la corporación para servir a mi comunidad y contribuir a la seguridad pública. Soy reciente egresado de la Licenciatura en Criminología y considero que tengo las habilidades y la vocación necesarias. Actualmente vivo con mi familia mientras completo este proceso de evaluación."
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save Motive">
    Click save.

    Success message: **"Se guardo motivo"**

    This counts as fulfilling the income requirement for Nuevo Ingreso.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices

### Preparation

1. **Start early**: Begin gathering documents as soon as you register
2. **Check expiration dates**: Renew expired documents immediately
3. **Order duplicates**: If documents are lost, start replacement process early
4. **Verify information**: Ensure names and details match across all documents
5. **Make copies**: Keep physical and digital copies for your records

### Quality Control

1. **Review before uploading**: Check that files are clear and complete
2. **Correct size and format**: Follow all technical requirements
3. **Complete documents**: Don't crop or cut off any part
4. **Proper orientation**: Rotate images to right-side up before uploading
5. **Test files**: Open files before uploading to ensure they're not corrupted

### Organization

1. **File naming**: Use clear names ("INE_Front.jpg", "INE_Back.jpg")
2. **Keep list**: Track which documents you've uploaded
3. **Save originals**: Keep original files in case re-upload is needed
4. **Document versions**: If you update a document, note which version was accepted

## Common Issues and Solutions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="File Too Large">
    **Problem**: File exceeds maximum size.

    **Solutions**:
    - Use PDF compression tools (online or software)
    - Reduce image resolution (but keep readable)
    - Split multi-page documents if allowed
    - Convert to PDF from images for better compression
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Wrong File Format">
    **Problem**: System doesn't accept file type.

    **Solutions**:
    - Convert to PDF (recommended for most documents)
    - Use JPG or PNG for photos and scanned images
    - Use free online converters if needed
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Document Rejected as Unclear">
    **Problem**: Upload was rejected due to quality.

    **Solutions**:
    - Re-scan with higher resolution
    - Use better lighting
    - Clean the scanner glass
    - Get a certified copy if original is poor quality
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Name Discrepancies">
    **Problem**: Name on one document doesn't match others.

    **Solutions**:
    - Get document corrected if it's an error
    - Include explanation in observations
    - Provide additional document showing name change (marriage certificate, etc.)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Missing Required Document">
    **Problem**: Don't have a required document.

    **Solutions**:
    - Birth Certificate: Request from Civil Registry
    - INE: Visit INE office for renewal
    - Proof of Address: Request from utility company
    - Proof of Studies: Contact your school
    - Military Card: Visit military recruitment office
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Upload Fails Repeatedly">
    **Problem**: Can't complete upload.

    **Solutions**:
    - Check internet connection stability
    - Try a different browser
    - Clear browser cache and cookies
    - Try smaller file size
    - Use a different device
    - Contact technical support
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
