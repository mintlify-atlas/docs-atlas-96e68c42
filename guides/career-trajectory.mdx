## Overview

The Career Trajectory section captures your complete work history. The specific information required differs significantly between **Permanencia** (existing employees) and **Nuevo Ingreso** (new hires).

<Warning>
This section has DIFFERENT forms based on your evaluation type. Make sure you understand which version applies to you.
</Warning>

## Evaluation Type Differences

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Permanencia" icon="user-check">
    **For existing employees:**
    - Current position details
    - Work history within CEACC/government
    - Multiple assignments/adscripciones
    - Legal and disciplinary history
    - Family in law enforcement
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Nuevo Ingreso" icon="user-plus">
    **For new hires:**
    - Desired position details
    - Previous private employment history
    - Multiple past jobs
    - How you learned about the position
    - Legal and disciplinary history
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Accessing Career Trajectory Form

From your evaluation dashboard, click on **Career Trajectory** (Trayectoria Laboral). You'll be directed to:

```
/captureData/trayectoriaCapture/[encrypted_evaluation_id]
```

The system automatically detects your evaluation type and shows the appropriate form.

## Part A: Permanencia (Existing Employees)

<Info>
This section applies if your evaluation type is Permanencia (tipoEvalID = 1).
</Info>

### Before You Begin

Gather information about:
- Your current position and department
- Employment start date
- Supervisor information
- Previous assignments within the organization
- Any legal proceedings or disciplinary actions
- Family members in law enforcement

### Step-by-Step: Permanencia

<Steps>
  <Step title="Current Position Information">
    **Dependencia** (`trayectoriaDependencia`)
    - Your current government agency or department
    - Example: "Secretaría de Seguridad Pública"

    **Adscripción** (`trayectoriaAdscripcion`)
    - Your specific unit or division
    - Example: "Dirección de Policía Estatal"

    **Cargo** (`trayectoriaCargo`)
    - Your job title/position
    - Example: "Oficial de Policía", "Inspector", "Comandante"

    **Grado** (`trayectoriaGrado`)
    - Your rank or grade level (if applicable)
    - Example: "Oficial 1", "Suboficial", "Comisario"
  </Step>

  <Step title="Employment Timeline">
    **Fecha de Ingreso** (`trayectoriaFechaIngreso`)
    - When you started working for the organization
    - Format: DD/MM/YYYY or as shown in form

    **Última Promoción** (`trayectoriaPromocion`)
    - Date of your most recent promotion
    - If never promoted, enter "N/A" or leave blank
  </Step>

  <Step title="Work Details">
    **Zona de Trabajo** (`trayectoriaZonaTrabajo`)
    - Geographic area where you work
    - Example: "Durango Capital", "Zona Norte", "Región Lagunar"

    **¿Porta Arma?** (`trayectoriaPortaArma`)
    - Do you carry a weapon?
    - Options: "Sí" or "No"

    **¿Tiene Personal a su Cargo?** (`trayectoriaPersonal`)
    - Do you supervise anyone?
    - Options: "Sí" or "No"

    **¿Cuántos?** (`trayectoriaCuantos`)
    - If yes, how many people do you supervise?
    - Enter a number
    - If no, enter "0" or "N/A"

    **Funciones Principales** (`trayectoriaFunciones`)
    - Describe your main job duties
    - Be specific and comprehensive
    - Example: "Patrullaje preventivo, atención a reportes ciudadanos, elaboración de informes"
  </Step>

  <Step title="Supervisor Information">
    **Nombre del Jefe Inmediato** (`trayectoriaNombreJefe`)
    - Full name of your direct supervisor

    **Puesto del Jefe** (`trayectoriaPuestoJefe`)
    - Your supervisor's job title
    - Example: "Comandante de Sector"

    **Teléfono del Jefe** (`trayectoriaNumJefe`)
    - Contact phone for your supervisor
    - Include extension if applicable
  </Step>

  <Step title="Legal and Disciplinary History">
    **¿Ha sido acusado, procesado, o sentenciado?** (`trayectoriaAcusaciones`)
    - Any criminal accusations or charges?
    - Options: "Sí" or "No"

    **¿Ha tenido procesos legales?** (`trayectoriaProcesos`)
    - Any legal proceedings?
    - Options: "Sí" or "No"

    **¿Ha recibido sanciones?** (`trayectoriaSanciones`)
    - Any disciplinary sanctions at work?
    - Options: "Sí" or "No"

    **¿Procedimientos administrativos?** (`trayectoriaProcedimientos`)
    - Any administrative proceedings?
    - Options: "Sí" or "No"

    **¿Quejas ciudadanas?** (`trayectoriaQuejas`)
    - Any citizen complaints filed against you?
    - Options: "Sí" or "No"

    <Info>
    If you answer "Sí" to any of these, you'll need to provide details.
    </Info>

    **Si respondió Sí, ¿Qué ocurrió?** (`trayectoriaOcurrioProcesos`)
    - Explain what happened
    - Be honest and complete

    **Motivo** (`trayectoriaMotivoProcesos`)
    - The reason or cause of the issue

    **Documentos** (`trayectoriaDocumentosProcesos`)
    - What documentation exists?
    - Examples: "Resolución absolutoria", "Carta de amonestación", "Acta administrativa"
  </Step>

  <Step title="C3 Evaluation History">
    **¿Cuándo fue su último C3?** (`trayectoriaUltimoCcc`)
    - Date of your most recent C3 evaluation
    - Format: Month and Year (e.g., "Enero 2024")

    **¿Dónde?** (`trayectoriaDondeCcc`)
    - Location where the evaluation was conducted
    - Example: "Centro de Control de Confianza Durango"

    **Dependencia** (`trayectoriaDependenciaCcc`)
    - Which agency requested the C3

    **Motivo** (`trayectoriaMotivoCcc`)
    - Reason for the evaluation
    - Examples: "Ingreso", "Permanencia", "Promoción"
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save Main Information">
    Click save to store your career trajectory data.

    Success message: **"Se guardaron datos laborales. Continua con Adscripciones"**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Previous Assignments (Adscripciones)">
    After saving, add your work history within the organization.

    For each previous assignment:

    **Nombre** (`adscripcionNombre`)
    - Name of the unit or department

    **Puesto** (`adscripcionPuesto`)
    - Your position title

    **Período** (`adscripcionPeriodo`)
    - Time period (e.g., "2018-2020")

    **Ingreso Mensual** (`adscripcionIngreso`)
    - Your monthly salary at that time

    **Motivo de Cambio** (`adscripcionMotivo`)
    - Why you left that position
    - Examples: "Promoción", "Reasignación", "Cambio de adscripción"

    **Nombre del Jefe** (`adscripcionJefe`)
    - Who was your supervisor

    <Info>
    You can add multiple assignments. Click save after each one.
    </Info>

    Success message per assignment: **"Se guardaron adscripciones"**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Family in Legal Proceedings (If Applicable)">
    If any family member has been involved in legal proceedings:

    **Nombre** (`famProcesoNombre`)
    - Family member's full name

    **Parentesco** (`famProcesoParentesco`)
    - Relationship to you

    **¿Cuándo ocurrió?** (`famProcesoCuando`)
    - When did it happen?

    **¿Qué ocurrió?** (`famProcesoQue`)
    - Description of what happened

    Click save for each family member.

    Success message: **"Se guardaro familiar con proceso"**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Family in Law Enforcement (If Applicable)">
    If you have family members working in law enforcement or government security:

    **Nombre** (`corpFamiliarNombre`)
    - Family member's name

    **Parentesco** (`corpFamiliarParentesco`)
    - Relationship to you

    <Note>
    This helps identify potential conflicts of interest or nepotism.
    </Note>

    Click save for each family member.

    Success message: **"Se guardaro familiar en coorporacion"**
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Part B: Nuevo Ingreso (New Hires)

<Info>
This section applies if your evaluation type is Nuevo Ingreso (tipoEvalID = 2).
</Info>

### Before You Begin

Gather information about:
- The position you're applying for
- How you learned about the vacancy
- Your previous employment history
- Any legal issues or proceedings
- Family in law enforcement

### Step-by-Step: Nuevo Ingreso

<Steps>
  <Step title="Position Information">
    **Puesto al que Evalúa** (`trayectoriaPuestoEval`)
    - The position you're applying for
    - Example: "Oficial de Policía", "Técnico en Criminología"

    **¿Cómo se enteró de la vacante?** (`trayectoriaEnteroVacante`)
    - How did you learn about this job opening?
    - Examples:
      - "Convocatoria pública"
      - "Recomendación de familiar"
      - "Sitio web de la dependencia"
      - "Redes sociales"
      - "Oficina de reclutamiento"
  </Step>

  <Step title="Understanding of Position">
    **Función 1** (`trayectoriaFuncion1`)
    - Describe one main function of the position

    **Función 2** (`trayectoriaFuncion2`)
    - Describe a second main function

    **Función 3** (`trayectoriaFuncion3`)
    - Describe a third main function

    <Tip>
    This shows you understand what the job entails. Be specific and realistic about the duties.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Legal and Disciplinary History">
    Same questions as Permanencia:

    - ¿Ha sido acusado, procesado, o sentenciado? (`trayectoriaAcusaciones`)
    - ¿Ha tenido procesos legales? (`trayectoriaProcesos`)
    - ¿Ha recibido sanciones? (`trayectoriaSanciones`)
    - ¿Procedimientos administrativos? (`trayectoriaProcedimientos`)
    - ¿Quejas ciudadanas? (`trayectoriaQuejas`)

    If yes to any:
    - ¿Qué ocurrió? (`trayectoriaOcurrio`)
    - Motivo (`trayectoriaMotivo`)
    - Documento de Resolución (`trayectoriaDocResolucion`)
  </Step>

  <Step title="C3 Evaluation History">
    Same as Permanencia:

    - ¿Cuándo fue su último C3? (`trayectoriaCcc`)
    - ¿Cuándo? (`trayectoriaCccCuando`)
    - Dependencia (`trayectoriaCccDependencia`)
    - Motivo (`trayectoriaCccMotivo`)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save Main Information">
    Click save to store your data.

    Success message: **"Se guardó trayectoria laboral NI!"**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Previous Employment History">
    Unlike Permanencia, Nuevo Ingreso requires listing previous private sector or other employment.

    For each previous job:

    **Empresa** (`empleoEmpresa`)
    - Name of the company or employer

    **Puesto** (`empleoPuesto`)
    - Your job title

    **Dirección** (`empleoDireccion`)
    - Address of the workplace

    **Período** (`empleoPeriodo`)
    - Time period worked (e.g., "2019-2021")

    **Ingreso Mensual** (`empleoIngreso`)
    - Your monthly salary

    **Motivo de Baja** (`empleoMotivo`)
    - Why you left the job
    - Examples: "Mejor oportunidad", "Fin de contrato", "Renuncia voluntaria"

    <Info>
    You can add multiple previous jobs. The system handles both single and multiple entries.
    </Info>

    Click save after entering all jobs.

    Success message: **"Se guardaron empleos"**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Family in Legal Proceedings (If Applicable)">
    Same as Permanencia - add family members involved in legal issues.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Family in Law Enforcement (If Applicable)">
    Same as Permanencia - add family members working in law enforcement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Career Trajectory Data

### Updating Your Information

**Permanencia:**
- Click update on the main form
- Make changes
- Save
- Success message: **"Se actualizaron datos laborales"**

**Nuevo Ingreso:**
- Click update on the main form
- Make changes
- Save
- Success message: **"Se actualizó trayectoria laboral NI!"**

### Deleting Entries

**Delete a Previous Job (Nuevo Ingreso):**
- Click delete next to the employment entry
- Confirm
- Success message: **"Se elimino empleo"**

**Delete All Career Trajectory Data:**
- From evaluation dashboard, delete the entire section
- Success message: **"Datos trayectoria eliminados"**
- This removes: main data, all assignments/jobs, family in legal proceedings, family in law enforcement

<Warning>
Deleting the entire section removes ALL career information including sub-sections. This cannot be undone.
</Warning>

## Best Practices

### For Permanencia Employees

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Listing Assignments">
    - Include all positions since you started
    - List in chronological order (oldest first or newest first - be consistent)
    - Include temporary assignments if they lasted more than a few months
    - Explain gaps in employment
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Supervisor Information">
    - Use your immediate supervisor (not skip-level)
    - Verify phone numbers are current
    - If supervisor has changed recently, use current supervisor
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Disciplinary History">
    - Be completely honest
    - If you were cleared, emphasize that
    - Provide context but don't make excuses
    - Have documentation ready
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### For Nuevo Ingreso Candidates

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Previous Employment">
    - List jobs in reverse chronological order (most recent first)
    - Include at least the last 5 years of employment
    - If you've never worked, explain (student, recent graduate, etc.)
    - Include part-time or informal work if significant
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Understanding the Position">
    - Research the position thoroughly before answering
    - Be realistic about duties (include difficult aspects)
    - Show you understand the responsibilities
    - Mention serving the community
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How You Learned About Job">
    - Be truthful about how you found out
    - If recommended by someone, mention who
    - Internal recommendations can be positive if disclosed
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Universal Tips

1. **Legal Issues**: Always disclose. Background checks will find them anyway.
2. **C3 History**: If you've never had a C3, enter "Ninguno" or "N/A"
3. **Family in Law Enforcement**: This is for disclosure, not a negative factor
4. **Be Thorough**: This section is critical for background investigation

## Section Completion

The Career Trajectory section is complete when:

**Permanencia:**
- Main career information saved
- At least one previous assignment added (if you've had any)
- Legal/disciplinary questions answered
- C3 history provided

**Nuevo Ingreso:**
- Main information saved (position, how you learned about it, functions)
- Previous employment history added (at least 1-2 jobs if you have work experience)
- Legal/disciplinary questions answered
- C3 history provided

## Troubleshooting

### Which form should I see?

**Problem:** Not sure if I'm seeing the right form.

**Solution:** Check your evaluation type from your profile or dashboard. The system should automatically load the correct form.

### Can't add multiple jobs/assignments

**Problem:** Only one job saved, others disappear.

**Solution:** Click save after EACH entry. Don't try to enter multiple at once without saving.

### Legal history questions confusing

**Problem:** Don't understand the difference between the questions.

**Solution:**
- **Acusaciones**: Were you accused of a crime?
- **Procesos**: Were you in a legal proceeding (civil or criminal)?
- **Sanciones**: Were you formally sanctioned at work?
- **Procedimientos**: Were administrative proceedings opened against you?
- **Quejas**: Did citizens file complaints about you?

When in doubt, answer "Yes" and explain in the details.
