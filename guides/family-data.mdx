## Overview

The Family Data section captures information about your immediate and extended family members. This includes parents, siblings, spouse, children, and any dependents.

<Info>
You'll need to add each family member individually. The system allows you to add as many family members as needed.
</Info>

## Accessing Family Data Form

From your evaluation dashboard, click on **Family Data** (Datos Familiares). You'll be directed to:

```
/captureData/familiaresCapture/[encrypted_evaluation_id]
```

## Before You Begin

For each family member, gather:

- Full name (first name and surnames)
- Relationship to you (parentesco)
- Occupation
- Age
- Whether they are still living (Vive: Sí/No)
- Phone number
- Whether they are your dependent (Dependiente económico: Sí/No)

<Note>
Include all immediate family members (parents, siblings, spouse, children). You may also include grandparents, in-laws, or other relatives who are significant in your life.
</Note>

## Adding Family Members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Family Member">
    From the Family Data page, click the button to add a new family member.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Names">
    **Apellidos** (`familiarApellidos`)
    - Enter the family member's surnames
    - Both paternal and maternal surnames
    - Example: "García López"

    **Nombres** (`familiarNombres`)
    - Enter the given name(s)
    - Example: "María Elena"

    <Info>
    The system automatically combines these into a full name: `familiarNombre = apellidos + nombres`
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Specify Relationship">
    **Parentesco** (`familiarParentesco`)
    - Select or enter the relationship
    - Common values:
      - Padre / Madre (Father / Mother)
      - Hermano/a (Brother / Sister)
      - Esposo/a (Husband / Wife)
      - Hijo/a (Son / Daughter)
      - Abuelo/a (Grandfather / Grandmother)
      - Suegro/a (Father-in-law / Mother-in-law)
      - Cuñado/a (Brother-in-law / Sister-in-law)
      - Tío/a (Uncle / Aunt)
      - Primo/a (Cousin)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Occupation and Age">
    **Ocupación** (`familiarOcupacion`)
    - Current occupation or job
    - For children in school: "Estudiante"
    - For retirees: "Jubilado/a" or "Pensionado/a"
    - For those not working: "Hogar" or "Desempleado/a"
    - For deceased: "Finado/a"

    **Edad** (`familiarEdad`)
    - Current age in years
    - Whole number
    - If deceased, age at death
  </Step>

  <Step title="Living Status">
    **¿Vive?** (`familiarVive`)
    - Select "Sí" if the person is alive
    - Select "No" if the person is deceased

    <Note>
    Even deceased immediate family members should be included (parents, siblings, spouse, children).
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Contact Information">
    **Teléfono** (`familiarTelefono`)
    - Phone number where the family member can be reached
    - Include area code
    - Format: 618-123-4567 or 6181234567
    - Leave empty if not applicable (deceased, no phone, etc.)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Dependent Status">
    **¿Es dependiente económico?** (`familiarDependiente`)
    - Select "Sí" if you provide financial support to this person
    - Select "No" if they are financially independent

    <Info>
    Common dependents include:
    - Children (especially minors)
    - Stay-at-home spouse
    - Elderly parents you support
    - Disabled family members you care for
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save Family Member">
    Click the save button.

    Success message: **"Se guardo familiar!"**

    The family member will appear in your list on the same page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add More Family Members">
    Repeat steps 1-7 for each family member.

    <Tip>
    Add family members in a logical order:
    1. Parents
    2. Spouse
    3. Children (oldest to youngest)
    4. Siblings
    5. Other relatives
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Family Members

### Viewing Your Family List

The page displays all family members you've added in a list or table showing:
- Full name
- Relationship
- Occupation
- Age
- Living status
- Phone number
- Dependent status

### Editing a Family Member

To update information:

1. Click the edit button next to the family member
2. Modify the fields as needed
3. Click save
4. Success message: **"Familiar actualizado!"**

<Note>
You can update any field except the evaluation ID. The family member's ID (`familiarID`) remains the same.
</Note>

### Deleting a Family Member

To remove a family member from the list:

1. Click the delete button next to the family member
2. Confirm the deletion
3. Success message: **"Familiar eliminado!"**

<Warning>
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You'll need to re-enter the family member if deleted by mistake.
</Warning>

## Deleting All Family Data

From the evaluation dashboard, you can delete your entire Family Data section:

1. Find the delete option for Family Data
2. Confirm deletion
3. All family members will be removed
4. Success message: **"Datos familiares eliminados"**

<Warning>
This removes ALL family members at once. Use with extreme caution.
</Warning>

## Best Practices

### Who to Include

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Always Include">
    - Both parents (even if deceased or estranged)
    - All siblings
    - Current spouse or partner
    - All children (biological, adopted, step-children)
    - Anyone who lives with you
    - Anyone you support financially
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Consider Including">
    - Parents-in-law
    - Grandparents (if significant relationship)
    - Siblings-in-law (if you interact regularly)
    - Ex-spouse (especially if co-parenting children)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Usually Not Needed">
    - Distant cousins
    - Aunts/uncles you rarely see
    - Extended family with no regular contact
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Accuracy Tips

1. **Use legal names**: Use full legal names, not nicknames
2. **Be consistent**: Use the same format for all entries
3. **Keep ages current**: Use current age, not age when you last saw them
4. **Phone numbers**: Use the most reliable contact number
5. **Dependent status**: Be honest about financial dependents

### Common Scenarios

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Deceased Family Member">
    - Set "¿Vive?" to "No"
    - Enter age at death
    - Occupation can be their last known job or "Finado/a"
    - Leave phone number empty
    - Dependent status: "No"
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Estranged Family Member">
    - Still include them if immediate family (parent, sibling)
    - Enter what information you know
    - "No" for dependent status
    - Can leave phone number empty if unknown
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Multiple Families (Remarriage)">
    - Include children from all relationships
    - Include current spouse
    - May include ex-spouse if you co-parent
    - Include step-children if you support them
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Minor Children">
    - Always mark as "Sí" for dependent
    - Occupation: "Estudiante" or "Menor de edad"
    - Phone can be your own or their school
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Adult Children">
    - Mark as dependent only if you still support them financially
    - Use their actual occupation
    - Use their own phone number
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Validation and Requirements

### Required Fields

For each family member, you must provide:
- Names (both `familiarNombres` and `familiarApellidos`)
- Relationship (`familiarParentesco`)
- Occupation (`familiarOcupacion`)
- Age (`familiarEdad`)
- Living status (`familiarVive`)
- Dependent status (`familiarDependiente`)

### Optional Fields

- Phone number (`familiarTelefono`)

<Info>
While phone is optional in the system, it's recommended to provide it whenever possible for living family members.
</Info>

## Section Completion

The Family Data section is considered complete when:

1. You have added at least one family member
2. All added family members have complete information
3. You have saved the section

<Note>
There is no specific minimum or maximum number of family members required. Add all relevant family members for a complete picture.
</Note>

## What Happens Next

After completing the Family Data section:

1. Your evaluation progress increases to approximately 33.33% (2 of 6 sections)
2. Return to your evaluation dashboard
3. Family Data section shows as complete ✓

## Troubleshooting

### Family member not saving

**Problem:** Click save but family member doesn't appear.

**Solution:** 
- Check that all required fields are filled
- Ensure names are not empty
- Try refreshing the page and re-entering

### Can't delete family member

**Problem:** Delete button not working.

**Solution:**
- Ensure you have the correct `familiarID`
- Check that you're logged into the right evaluation
- Try refreshing the page

### Lost family data after editing

**Problem:** Made changes but they disappeared.

**Solution:**
- Always click save after editing
- Don't navigate away before saving
- If lost, re-enter the information
