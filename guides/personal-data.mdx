## Overview

The Personal Data section is the first of six data sections you need to complete. This section captures your basic personal information, contact details, residential address, and social media profiles.

<Info>
This is typically the first section you should complete, as some information here may be referenced in other sections.
</Info>

## Accessing Personal Data Form

From your evaluation dashboard, click on the **Personal Data** (Datos Personales) section. You'll be directed to:

```
/captureData/personalesCapture/[encrypted_evaluation_id]
```

Gather the following information:

<AccordionGroup>

## Completing the Personal Data Form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Basic Personal Information">
    Fill in the following fields:

    **Nombre Completo** (`personalesNombre`)
    - Your full legal name
    - Must match official documents

    **Fecha de Nacimiento** (`personalesFechaNac`)
    - Date format: YYYY-MM-DD or as shown in form

    **Lugar de Nacimiento** (`personalesLugarNac`)
    - City and state where you were born
    - Example: "Durango, Durango"

    **Nacionalidad** (`personalesNacionalidad`)
    - Usually "Mexicana" or "Mexicano"

    **Sexo** (`personalesSexo`)
    - Must match your registration

    **Estado Civil** (`personalesEdoCivil`)
    - Soltero/a, Casado/a, Divorciado/a, Viudo/a, Unión Libre

    **Edad** (`personalesEdad`)
    - Your current age in years

    **Escolaridad** (`personalesEscolaridad`)
    - Your highest level of education completed
  </Step>

  <Step title="Official Identification Numbers">
    **RFC** (`personalesRFC`)
    - Your RFC with homoclave (13 characters)
    - Should match your registration RFC

    **CURP** (`personalesCURP`)
    - Your 18-character CURP
    - Must be valid and match your name/birthdate
  </Step>

  <Step title="Contact Information">
    **Teléfono Fijo** (`personalesTelFijo`)
    - Home landline phone number
    - Include area code
    - Can be left empty if you don't have one

    **Teléfono Celular** (`personalesTelCelular`)
    - Your primary mobile phone
    - Must be reachable for verification

    **Teléfono Oficina** (`personalesTelOficina`)
    - Your work phone number

    **Extensión** (`personalesExt`)
    - Office phone extension if applicable

    **Teléfono de Recados** (`personalesTelRecados`)
    - Alternative contact phone number
    - For someone who can reach you

    **Persona de Recados** (`personalesPersonaRecados`)
    - Name of the person at the alternate contact number
    - Their relationship to you

    **Correo Electrónico** (`personalesEmail`)
    - Your email address
    - Can be the same as your login username
  </Step>

  <Step title="Residential Address">
    **Calle y Número** (`personalesCalleNum`)
    - Full street address with house/apartment number
    - Example: "Av. Francisco Villa #123"

    **Colonia** (`personalesColonia`)
    - Neighborhood or colony name

    **Código Postal** (`personalesCP`)
    - 5-digit postal code

    **Localidad** (`personalesLocalidad`)
    - City or locality name

    **Estado** (`personalesEstado`)
    - State name
    - Example: "Durango"

    **Años en Domicilio** (`personalesAnioDom`)
    - How many years you've lived at this address
    - Whole number (e.g., "5" for 5 years)

    **Personas que Habitan** (`personalesPersonasHab`)
    - Total number of people living in the household
    - Include yourself in the count

    **Entre Calles** (`personalesEntreCalles`)
    - Cross streets for reference
    - Example: "Entre Calle Juárez y Calle Hidalgo"

    **Descripción de Fachada** (`personalesFachada`)
    - Describe your home's exterior
    - Color, material, distinctive features
    - Example: "Casa de dos pisos, color blanco, portón negro"

    **Google Maps** (`personalesGoogle`)
    - Optional: Paste a Google Maps link to your address
    - Helps investigators locate your residence
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save Personal Information">
    Click the save button to store your personal data.

    The system will:
    - Validate required fields
    - Save data to `tbl_dgo_personales` table
    - Show success message: **"Datos personales guardados, captura redes sociales"**
    - Keep you on the same page to add social media
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Social Media Profiles (Optional)">
    After saving personal data, you can add social media accounts:

    **Tipo de Red** (`redesTipoRed`)
    - Select the social media platform
    - Options: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, etc.

    **Usuario** (`redesUsuario`)
    - Your username or profile URL on that platform

    Click save to add each social media account.

    <Info>
    You can add multiple social media accounts by repeating this process. Each account is saved separately in the `tbl_dgo_redes_sociales` table.
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing Personal Data

If you need to update your information:

1. Return to the Personal Data section from your evaluation dashboard
2. The form will be pre-populated with your existing data
3. Make your changes
4. Click the update button
5. Success message: **"Datos personales actualizados"**

<Warning>
Changing your RFC or CURP may cause validation issues. These should match your official documents exactly.
</Warning>

## Managing Social Media Profiles

### Adding More Profiles

You can add as many social media accounts as needed:
1. Select the platform type
2. Enter your username
3. Click save
4. Repeat for other platforms

### Deleting Profiles

To remove a social media account:
1. Find the profile in your list
2. Click the delete button
3. Confirm deletion
4. Success message: **"Red social borrada"**

## Deleting All Personal Data

From the evaluation dashboard, you can delete your entire Personal Data section:

1. Look for the delete option in Personal Data
2. Confirm you want to delete
3. All personal data and social media profiles will be removed
4. Success message: **"Datos personales eliminados"**
5. You'll need to re-enter everything from scratch

<Warning>
This action cannot be undone. All personal data including social media profiles will be permanently deleted.
</Warning>

## Validation Tips

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="RFC Format Errors">
    **Incorrect:** ABCD850101 (missing homoclave)
    
    **Correct:** ABCD850101XYZ (13 characters total)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="CURP Errors">
    **Incorrect:** ABCD850101HDFXYZ01 (17 characters)
    
    **Correct:** ABCD850101HDFXYZ012 (18 characters)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Phone Number Format">
    Include area codes:
    - Mobile: 618-123-4567 or 6181234567
    - Landline: 618-123-4567
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Address Details">
    Be specific:
    - Good: "Calle Constitución #456, Interior 3"
    - Too vague: "Centro"
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Required vs Optional Fields

**Required Fields:**
- All basic personal information (name, birth date, etc.)
- RFC and CURP
- Mobile phone
- Email
- Complete residential address

**Optional Fields:**
- Home phone (landline)
- Office phone and extension
- Alternative contact phone
- Google Maps link
- Social media profiles
