## Overview

The Academic Data section captures your educational history, including your highest degree earned and any additional professional training, certifications, or courses you've completed.

<Info>
This section has two parts:
1. **Highest Academic Degree** (required) - Your formal education
2. **Additional Training/Certifications** (optional but recommended) - Professional development courses
</Info>

## Accessing Academic Data Form

From your evaluation dashboard, click on **Academic Data** (Datos Académicos). You'll be directed to:

```
/captureData/academicosCapture/[encrypted_evaluation_id]
```

## Before You Begin

Gather information about:

### Your Highest Degree
- Degree type (Primaria, Secundaria, Preparatoria, Licenciatura, Maestría, Doctorado)
- Institution name
- Start and end dates (or period)
- Supporting document type (certificate, diploma, transcript)
- Document location

### Professional Training (if applicable)
- Course or certification name
- Training institution
- Start and end dates
- Type of document received (certificate, diploma, constancia)

<Note>
Have your educational documents ready. You'll need to reference them and eventually upload them in the Documents section.
</Note>

## Part 1: Highest Academic Degree

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter Degree Information">
    **Último Grado** (`academicoUltimoGrado`)
    - Select or enter your highest completed level of education:
      - Primaria (Elementary School)
      - Secundaria (Middle School)
      - Preparatoria / Bachillerato (High School)
      - Carrera Técnica (Technical Career)
      - Licenciatura (Bachelor's Degree)
      - Maestría (Master's Degree)
      - Doctorado (Doctorate/PhD)

    <Info>
    Select your **highest completed** degree. If you're currently pursuing a degree but haven't graduated, list the highest degree you've already completed.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Specify Time Period">
    **Período Inicio** (`academicoPeriodoInicio`)
    - When you started this educational program
    - Format: Year or full date
    - Example: "2015" or "Agosto 2015"

    **Período Final** (`academicoPeriodoFinal`)
    - When you completed/graduated
    - Format: Year or full date
    - Example: "2019" or "Julio 2019"

    <Tip>
    If you only remember the years, that's sufficient. Exact months are helpful but not mandatory.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Institution Details">
    **Institución** (`academicoInstitucion`)
    - Full name of the school, college, or university
    - Include the campus if there are multiple locations
    - Examples:
      - "Universidad Juárez del Estado de Durango"
      - "Instituto Tecnológico de Durango"
      - "Colegio de Bachilleres del Estado de Durango"
  </Step>

  <Step title="Document Information">
    **Tipo de Documento** (`academicoDocumento`)
    - What proof of completion you have:
      - Título (Degree/Title)
      - Cédula Profesional (Professional License)
      - Certificado (Certificate)
      - Diploma
      - Constancia (Letter of Completion)
      - Boleta de Calificaciones (Transcript)

    **Ubicación del Documento** (`academicoUbicacion`)
    - Where the physical document is located
    - Examples:
      - "En mi domicilio"
      - "En oficina de recursos humanos"
      - "En trámite de expedición"
      - "Extraviado" (if lost)

    <Warning>
    You will need to upload this document later in the Documents section. Make sure you know where it is or can obtain a copy.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save Degree Information">
    Click the save button to store your highest academic degree.

    Success message: **"Último grado académico guardado"**

    The system saves this to the `tbl_dgo_academicos` table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Part 2: Additional Training & Certifications

After saving your highest degree, you can add professional training courses, workshops, certifications, or diplomas.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Training">
    Look for the section to add additional training or capacitaciones.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Course Details">
    **Detalle de Capacitación** (`capacitacionDetalle`)
    - Full name of the course, workshop, or certification
    - Be specific
    - Examples:
      - "Curso de Derechos Humanos y Uso de la Fuerza"
      - "Certificación en Cadena de Custodia"
      - "Diplomado en Investigación Criminal"
      - "Taller de Ética Policial"
  </Step>

  <Step title="Training Period">
    **Inicio** (`capacitacionInicio`)
    - Start date of the training
    - Can be year, month/year, or full date

    **Final** (`capacitacionFinal`)
    - Completion date
    - Use same format as start date

    <Info>
    For single-day workshops, you can use the same date for both inicio and final.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Training Institution">
    **Institución** (`capacitacionInstitucion`)
    - Organization that provided the training
    - Examples:
      - "Centro de Control de Confianza"
      - "Secretaría de Seguridad Pública"
      - "Instituto Nacional de Ciencias Penales"
      - Name of private training company
  </Step>

  <Step title="Proof of Completion">
    **Tipo de Documento** (`capacitacionDocumento`)
    - What you received upon completion:
      - Constancia (Certificate of Attendance)
      - Diploma
      - Certificado (Certification)
      - Reconocimiento (Recognition)

    <Note>
    Some trainings may not provide documentation. If this is the case, note "Sin documento" or "Verbal" and explain in observations if asked.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save Training">
    Click save to add this training to your list.

    Success message: **"Capacitación guardada!"**

    The training is saved to `tbl_dgo_capacitaciones` table.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add More Training (Optional)">
    Repeat steps 1-5 for each additional training, course, or certification.

    <Tip>
    Add the most relevant and recent training first. Focus on:
    - Required certifications for your position
    - Recent professional development (last 5 years)
    - Specialized training related to your duties
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Academic Data

### Viewing Your Academic Information

The page displays:
- Your highest academic degree
- A list of all training/certifications you've added

### Updating Your Degree Information

To edit your highest academic degree:

1. The form will show your existing degree information
2. Make any necessary changes
3. Click update/save
4. Success message: **"Se actualizo grado academico"**

### Editing a Training Entry

To modify a training record:

1. Click the edit button next to the training
2. Update the fields
3. Click save
4. Success message: **"Se actualizó capacitación"**

### Deleting Entries

**Delete a Training/Certification:**
1. Click delete next to the training
2. Confirm deletion
3. Success message: **"Se eliminó capacitación"**

**Delete Your Degree:**
1. Use the delete option for the degree section
2. Confirm deletion
3. Success message: **"Se eliminó grado academico"**

<Warning>
Deleting your academic degree removes it completely. You'll need to re-enter all information.
</Warning>

## Deleting All Academic Data

From the evaluation dashboard, you can delete the entire Academic Data section:

1. Find the delete option for Academic Data
2. Confirm deletion
3. Both degree and all training records will be removed
4. Success message: **"Datos academicos eliminados"**

<Warning>
This removes EVERYTHING: your degree and all training/certifications. Use with extreme caution.
</Warning>

## Best Practices

### What Training to Include

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Definitely Include">
    - Required certifications for your position
    - C3 evaluations and certifications
    - Police academy or similar foundational training
    - Specialized law enforcement training
    - Recent courses (last 5 years)
    - Any training with official certification
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Consider Including">
    - Relevant workshops and seminars
    - Online courses with certificates
    - In-house training programs
    - First aid, CPR, or emergency response training
    - Language courses
    - Computer skills training
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Usually Not Needed">
    - Very brief orientations (less than 4 hours)
    - Informal team meetings
    - Training without any documentation or verification
    - Irrelevant personal hobbies or interests
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Accuracy Tips

1. **Verify dates**: Check your documents for accurate dates
2. **Use official names**: Use the institution's full legal name
3. **Be specific**: "Diplomado en Investigación" is better than "Curso"
4. **Document types matter**: Know the difference between constancia, certificado, and diploma
5. **Keep records accessible**: You'll need to upload proof of education later

### Common Scenarios

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Incomplete Degree">
    **Situation:** Started university but didn't finish.
    
    **Solution:** List your highest completed degree (likely Preparatoria). Don't list incomplete degrees.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Foreign Degree">
    **Situation:** Degree from another country.
    
    **Solution:** 
    - List the degree type equivalent in Mexico
    - Include the country in the institution name
    - Note if it's been validated/revalidated in México
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Multiple Degrees">
    **Situation:** Have both Licenciatura and Maestría.
    
    **Solution:** List only your highest (Maestría) in the degree section. You can mention the Licenciatura in capacitaciones if relevant.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Lost Documents">
    **Situation:** Can't find your certificate.
    
    **Solution:** 
    - Enter "Extraviado" in document location
    - Contact your institution about getting a duplicate
    - You may need this for document upload later
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Currently Studying">
    **Situation:** Pursuing a degree right now.
    
    **Solution:** List your highest completed degree. Don't include current studies unless you've officially graduated.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Validation and Requirements

### Required: Highest Academic Degree

You MUST complete:
- Último Grado (`academicoUltimoGrado`)
- Período Inicio (`academicoPeriodoInicio`)
- Período Final (`academicoPeriodoFinal`)
- Institución (`academicoInstitucion`)
- Tipo de Documento (`academicoDocumento`)
- Ubicación (`academicoUbicacion`)

### Optional: Training & Certifications

While not mandatory, adding relevant training strengthens your evaluation. Each training entry requires:
- Detalle (`capacitacionDetalle`)
- Inicio and Final dates
- Institución (`capacitacionInstitucion`)
- Documento (`capacitacionDocumento`)

## Connection to Documents Section

Remember: You will need to upload proof of your education in the [Document Requirements](/guides/document-requirements) section:

- **Permanencia**: Comprobante de Estudios (required)
- **Nuevo Ingreso**: Comprobante de Estudios (required)

Make sure the degree you list here matches the document you'll upload.

## Section Completion

The Academic Data section is considered complete when:

1. You have entered your highest academic degree
2. All required fields for the degree are filled
3. You have saved the information

<Info>
Training/certifications are optional but recommended. Even without any additional training, the section can be marked complete if the degree is saved.
</Info>

## What Happens Next

## Troubleshooting

### Can't save degree information

**Problem:** Save button not working.

**Solution:**
- Ensure all required fields are filled
- Check date formats are valid
- Try refreshing the page

### Training not appearing in list

**Problem:** Added training but don't see it.

**Solution:**
- Verify you clicked save
- Refresh the page
- Check you're on the correct evaluation

### Which degree to list?

**Problem:** Unsure which of multiple degrees to enter.

**Solution:** Always list your **highest completed** degree. If you have multiple degrees at the same level (e.g., two Licenciaturas), list the most recent or most relevant to your current position.
